At **EADH2026 in Kraków**, we present our conference paper and poster on
creating Heritage Digital Twins with open-source tools.

Our work starts from a practical problem: knowledge about a cultural object is
often dispersed across museum collections, archives, libraries, research
publications, photographs, websites, and different languages. Rather than
replacing these sources with a single database or ontology, we investigate how
they can be connected into **reviewable and interoperable digital
representations**.

The conference paper develops the methodological approach. We treat the Heritage
Digital Twin not simply as a model of a heritage object, but as a research
environment in which documentary evidence can be brought together, claims can be
reviewed, and provenance, uncertainty, rights, and governance can remain
explicit.
